About this sticker

1. Add income or a new expense: tap the heading to open the form. Every entry asks you to confirm first. The opening balance is set once, the first time.

2. Double-tap the balance number to correct it. Enter the new balance and an optional reason; the difference is saved as an Adjustment.

3. Newest entries are on top. Delete removes one entry after confirmation, unless that would push the balance past the limit.

4. c copies the month and balance. Exit shows a blank screen; tap anywhere to reopen.

5. Entries older than 36 days, and the oldest entry once you reach 50, move into “Carried forward”. The balance does not change.

6. Everything stays on this phone. Browsers may clear it after long disuse, so export a backup now and then (you will be reminded after 30 days). Import replaces all current data.

7. Limits: CA$2,000 per amount and balance, 50 records, 20 characters per memo.
