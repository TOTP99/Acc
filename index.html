Help

1. What this is
A ledger for income and expenses. Passwords are stored as hashes only, never as plain text. Data stays on this device and is never uploaded.

2. Getting started
Pick whether you are payer or payee, then set a password: 6 characters with exactly 2 letters and 4 digits (letters are case-sensitive). The other account can be added later from the sign-in screen or Settings.

3. Add Entry
Tap "+ Add Entry": choose Income or Expense, fill in date, category and amount. A memo is optional. Tap Submit.

4. Income vs expense
Income posts right away. An expense stays "Pending Approval" and does not count in expense totals until the other account approves it.

5. How approval works
The other account opens Pending, taps Approve to post or Reject to leave it out. Rejected entries stay in the list. You cannot approve your own expense.

6. Viewing
Use the arrows to change months, or tap the month title to pick a year and month. Tap any row for full details: who submitted it, who approved it, and when.

7. Notifications
After an entry, a notification message pops up. Copy it, or send it by SMS or email. The Notifications tab also sends messages; the other side sees read status and read time.

8. Inviting the other account
Tap Invite on the sign-in screen or in Settings. The other phone scans the QR code, opens the same web address, and signs in with their own account. The QR code needs internet once; bookkeeping itself works offline.

9. Old records
In Settings, drag the slider to set the retention period (1–12 months, default 3). Older details are deleted automatically, but the latest income and latest expense are always kept, and balances carry forward, so Total Balance never changes. Cleanup never touches accounts, passwords, notifications, or settings.

10. Opening balance, backup, password, sign out
Settings holds the opening balance (money from before bookkeeping started), Export Backup (a file with everything, including opening balance), Import Backup (bring a file in when switching phones), and Change Password. Sign Out is at the top right. Every save also makes an automatic backup; Data Security can restore the latest one, and every cleanup is logged there. Statements print or save as PDF, and CSV opens in spreadsheet apps. The backup file is for restore.
